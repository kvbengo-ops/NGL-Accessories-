Delivery & Returns

Delivery

We currently serve Metro Cebu only — from Cebu City up to Consolacion, including Mandaue City, Liloan, and Compostela. At checkout you can choose Meetup (free — we agree on a spot and time) or Maxim / Movit delivery, where the rider's fare is paid directly to the rider on arrival. Orders are usually ready within 1–2 days, and we'll message you to coordinate.

Returns & Exchanges

Unworn items in original condition may be returned or exchanged within 7 days of delivery. To start a return, email support@nglaccessories.com with your order number. Return delivery is shouldered by the customer unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is your jewelry waterproof?

Yes — our pieces are designed to be water-resistant for everyday wear, including handwashing and light sweat. We still recommend removing them before swimming in chlorinated pools or the sea to maximise their lifespan.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept GCash, bank transfer, and cash (paid in person at meetup or on delivery). For GCash or bank transfer, enter your reference number or upload a receipt at checkout so we can confirm it.

How do I know my order went through?

You'll see an order confirmation screen with your order number right after checkout. You can also look it up anytime on the order tracking page using your order number and email.

How long does delivery take?

Within Metro Cebu (Cebu City up to Consolacion), meetups and Maxim/Movit deliveries are usually arranged within 1–2 days. We'll message you to coordinate a time, and you can follow your order on the order tracking page.
