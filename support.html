Shipping & Returns

Shipping

We ship nationwide across the Philippines. Orders are processed within 1–2 business days and typically arrive in 3–5 business days. Shipping is a flat ₱150, and free for orders over ₱1,500.

Returns & Exchanges

Unworn items in original condition may be returned or exchanged within 7 days of delivery. To start a return, email support@nglaccessories.com with your order number. Return shipping is shouldered by the customer unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is your jewelry waterproof?

Yes — our pieces are designed to be water-resistant for everyday wear, including handwashing and light sweat. We still recommend removing them before swimming in chlorinated pools or the sea to maximise their lifespan.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept GCash and bank transfer. After placing your order, enter your payment reference number so we can confirm it.

How do I know my order went through?

You'll see an order confirmation screen with your order number right after checkout. You can also look it up anytime on the order tracking page using your order number and email.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive within 3–5 business days after processing. You'll be notified as your order moves through processing and shipping.
